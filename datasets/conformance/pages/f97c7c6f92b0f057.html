Garlic-cucumber yogurt dip (τζατζίκι)

Grate one cucumber, salt it, and squeeze it bone-dry. Fold into thick Greek yogurt with grated garlic, olive oil, a splash of red-wine vinegar, and chopped dill. Your fine-edged knife makes quick work of the dill.
