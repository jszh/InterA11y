Permit application

Residents of permit zones A–F may apply for up to two annual permits per household. Have your vehicle registration and a recent utility bill ready.

Contact & office hours

Permit Office, 200 County Plaza, Riverside. Open Mon–Fri 8:30am–4:30pm. Phone 555-0142. Closed on county holidays.

This page is for parking permits only. For business permits, visit the Licensing Office service.
