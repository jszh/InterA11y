The Industrial Revolution: causes, costs, contested narratives

Module 6 · Due Friday 11:59 PM

This week’s reading

We move from the agrarian world into the factory age. The single most important shift to keep in mind is the move from organic to mineral energy. Start with the Mokyr chapter on the British lead, then read the Berg essay on women and work. Note that Mokyr argues technology was necessary but not sufficient; Berg complicates the heroic-inventor story entirely.

Discussion prompt

For the seminar, post a 200-word response to the Week 6 discussion thread. Engage with at least one classmate. The most common mistake here is treating “industrialisation” as a single event — it was uneven, regional, and slow. If you want a counterpoint to Mokyr, the optional Allen reading on high wages is short and worth your time.
