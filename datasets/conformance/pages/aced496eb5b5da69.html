Renew your resident parking permit

Permits run for twelve months. You can renew online up to 30 days before your current permit expires.

Before you start

  1. Confirm your vehicle is still registered to your home address. If it isn’t, update it first through the change-of-address service.
  2. Have last year’s permit number ready. You can look it up in your permit history if you’ve lost the sticker.
  3. Check that your zone still allows residential permits — a few streets moved to metered-only this year, listed in the 2026 zone changes notice.
  4. Pay the £45 fee by card. Concessions for blue-badge holders are applied automatically; details are on the concessions page.
