Residents & Services

Find everyday city services — pay bills, request pickups, and apply for permits. Most online services are available 24/7.

Welcome to Brookhaven's resident services hub. Use the menu above to jump to a service, or browse the featured links below.

The "Residents & Services" submenu opens on hover and on activation/focus of its toggle. Once open it covers the page banner and intro text. The only way it closes is a document click outside it (mouse) — there is deliberately no Esc handler and no toggle-to-close on the trigger, so a keyboard or magnifier user cannot dismiss the obscuring panel without moving focus away.
