Residential parking permit

Apply for a permit for a vehicle registered at your address in Zones A–E.

Your zone is printed on the renewal letter, top-right, as “Zone A–E”. No letter? Look up your street in the Zone finder on this page. Press Esc to close.

Hover or focus the ? beside “Parking zone” for help. The help text stays open while you move onto it, and Esc closes it.
