Enable two-factor authentication

Account & security · 3 min read · Updated March 2024

  1. Open your security settings

    Click your avatar in the top-right corner, then choose Settings. In the left sidebar, select Security.

  2. Turn on two-factor authentication

    In the Security panel, find the Two-factor authentication row and click the Turn on button on the right.

  3. Scan the code with your authenticator app

    A QR code will appear. Open your authenticator app, scan it, and enter the 6-digit code to confirm.

    Tip: save your backup codes somewhere safe in case you lose your phone.
