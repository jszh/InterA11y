How we cut our cold-brew steep time in half

By Priya Raman · Updated 14 June 2026 · 6 min read

When we re-tooled the roastery’s batch process last spring, the bottleneck was obvious: a full eighteen hours of immersion before a single bottle could be filled. We started by re-reading our own grind-distribution notes from 2023, then tore apart the assumptions in our extraction-curve write-up, which had quietly become gospel without anyone re-testing it.

The short version: particle size, not time, was doing most of the work. A coarser, more even grind pulled the same total dissolved solids in nine hours that an uneven medium grind needed eighteen to reach. If you want the raw numbers, they live in our 2026 trial spreadsheet, refractometer readings and all.

“We had been paying for time we didn’t need to spend. The grinder was the lever the whole time.”

None of this is new science — folks at the Specialty Coffee Association have been saying it for years. What changed for us was finally measuring instead of trusting a steep schedule we’d inherited from the previous owners.
