Glossary v3.2

Terms used throughout the Pulsar Payments REST reference. Cross-references point to the canonical definition or the relevant endpoint guide.

Idempotency key
A client-generated Idempotency-Key header that lets you safely retry a request without applying the operation twice. Keys are scoped to a single merchant account and expire after 24 hours. See also the retry behaviour guide.
Settlement batch
The group of captured charges that are paid out together on a given banking day. Each batch references one payout account; timing follows the schedule in the payouts guide.
Webhook signature
An HMAC-SHA256 digest sent in the Pulsar-Signature header so you can verify a callback originated from us. Rotate the secret from the webhooks dashboard and validate it as described under replay window.
Replay window
The five-minute tolerance during which a signed webhook is accepted, after which a replayed request is rejected. Tune it alongside your idempotency key strategy.
