Finding Your Way to the Venues

The Frostvale Winter Games are spread across five competition venues in and around the city. The map below shows where each venue sits relative to the city centre and the main rail line. Plan extra travel time on competition mornings.

Volunteers in teal jackets are stationed at every rail exit to help with directions. Shuttle buses run between the rail station and each venue every fifteen minutes.
