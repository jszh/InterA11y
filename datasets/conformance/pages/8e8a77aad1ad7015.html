Harbor Commission Approves Long-Delayed Ferry Terminal

By Renata Volk · Marine & Transit Desk · 7 min read

Construction is scheduled to begin in late spring.

After three years of permitting disputes, the Harbor Commission voted 5–2 on Tuesday night to fund the replacement ferry terminal at Pier 9, ending a standoff that had stalled commuter service to the outer islands. The new structure will add two berths and a covered waiting concourse.

Commissioners said the project would be paid for through a mix of state transit grants and a modest fare surcharge phased in over four years. Opponents argued the surcharge would fall hardest on island residents who have no alternative route to the mainland.
