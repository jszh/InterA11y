Welcome to BIOL 204

This course covers membrane transport, the central dogma, and the regulation of gene expression. Lectures meet Tuesday and Thursday in Whitcomb Hall 118. Lab sections begin in week 2.

Use the tabs above to reach the syllabus and the weekly readings. Office hours are posted in the syllabus tab.

Syllabus & course policies

The grading breakdown is 40% exams, 25% labs, 20% problem sets, and 15% participation. Late problem sets lose 10% per day. Academic-integrity policy applies to all submitted work.
