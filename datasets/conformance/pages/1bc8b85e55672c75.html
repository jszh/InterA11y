City Hall protest draws thousands over transit-fare hike

By Dana Okafor, Staff Reporter · Updated 6:42 p.m.

Thousands of transit riders converged on City Hall on Tuesday to protest a proposed 40-cent increase to the standard bus and rail fare, packing the plaza and spilling onto Fourth Street as a brass band played and organizers led chants from a flatbed truck.

Council members are scheduled to vote on the fare package Thursday. Transit authority figures show ridership has not recovered to pre-2020 levels, and officials say the increase is needed to close a projected $31 million operating gap.

"People here depend on the 12 line to get to work," said organizer Priya Venkat, gesturing at the crowd. "A fare hike is a pay cut for everyone who can't afford to drive."
