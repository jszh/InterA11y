Resawing a 10" walnut slab without a bandsaw — is a track saw + handsaw realistic?

I scored a kiln-dried walnut slab but my bandsaw maxes out at 6" resaw height. I've seen people score both faces with a track saw and finish the middle with a frame saw. Anyone done this on hardwood that thick? How flat did it come out?

@flatsawn_fred · 5h ago · ▲ 42

Done it on 8/4 maple. Score 1/8" deep both faces as a kerf guide, then a 700mm frame saw. Expect to flatten ~1/16" of cup afterward. Worth it.

@sawdust_sue · 4h ago · ▲ 11

Track saw from both sides only gets you ~110mm combined on most rails — for a true 10" slab you'll still have a fat middle to handsaw. Mark a center line and go slow.
