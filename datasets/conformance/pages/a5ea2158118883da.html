Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Ferndale Credit Union collects about you, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers the data you give us when you open an account, the device and usage data we collect when you sign in to Online Banking, and the limited circumstances in which we share information with payment networks and regulators.

You can ask us to export or delete your personal data at any time, opt out of marketing analytics, and review the third-party processors we rely on. We never sell your personal information. Questions about your data should go to our Data Protection Officer at dpo@ferndale.example.

Terms of Service

These Terms of Service govern your use of Ferndale Online Banking and the mobile app. By signing in you agree to keep your credentials confidential, to use the service only for lawful transactions on accounts you own, and to notify us within two business days of any unauthorised activity.

We may suspend access for suspected fraud, impose daily transfer limits, and amend these Terms with 30 days' notice. Liability for service interruptions is limited as described in section 9. Disputes are handled under the laws of the chartering province.
