About these metrics

Throughput figures are sampled over a sliding 60-second window. The p95 latency reflects the end-to-end time from event acceptance at the edge collector to durable commit in the warehouse. Degraded pipelines continue to process data but have breached at least one of their configured service-level objectives in the last 15 minutes.

To change which metrics appear on this dashboard or the auto-refresh interval, use the Dashboard settings control in the toolbar above. Settings are stored per workspace and apply to everyone with access to this view.
