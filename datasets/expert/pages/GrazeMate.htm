How GrazeMate Works.

01

Install GrazeMate on your Farm

This includes the DJI drone + base station, processing unit, and power source (Solar or AC). 

02

Schedule a Shift in the App

In the app, you can see all the paddock boundaries on your ranch. Simply select the required field, gate, and time for an upcoming muster.

03

Set the Fields + Gates

Before GrazeMate takes off, you will be asked to confirm the correct gates have been opened, and the new paddock is ready.

04

Monitor as GrazeMate Gathers.

From here, GrazeMate will autonomously fly to the selected paddock and start the move. Everything can be monitored and tuned from our app.

FAQs: Get the Answers You Need to Know

Does GrazeMate work without phone signal or internet?

Yes. GrazeMate automatically toggles between a network connection (when mobile coverage is available) and a local mode, which activates when you’re within 20 m of the base station. That means you can operate seamlessly in remote areas without worrying about connectivity.

How is GrazeMate powered?

We offer two power options: one connects directly to AC mains, and another runs off a solar panel with a backup battery for remote deployment. Each unit covers up to 10 km on a single charge and recharges in about 30 minutes, keeping you running all day.

Can it handle trees, hills, and rough terrain?

Yes. GrazeMate’s sensors and AI are trained for real-world conditions — from open paddocks to wooded or uneven terrain — maintaining stable tracking even when visibility drops.

How large a herd can GrazeMate manage?

Using a single drone, we typically see strong control and tracking for herds of up to 2,000 animals. It can scale beyond that, though precision becomes harder to maintain across very large groups simultaneously.

What happens if a unit loses power or signal?

No data is lost — GrazeMate stores information locally and automatically syncs when reconnected to the network or base station.

How does GrazeMate reduce mustering costs and stress?

Farmers often spend tens or hundreds of thousands of dollars mustering each year. GrazeMate cuts this roughly in half. By analysing animal biomarkers, our AI minimises stress in cattle — trained with insights from some of the world’s best stockmen.

How do I get started with GrazeMate?

We’re currently rolling out pilot programs and conducting extensive testing. Join the waitlist and send us a message — we’ll begin onboarding you into a pilot and provide a demo unit for testing.
