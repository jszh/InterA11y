Our Process

MORSE applies a user centered development process. We continuously engage with our users to better understand their needs and gather feedback as a project progresses. We pride ourselves in our hands-on approach to field testing alongside users in operationally representative conditions.


This process results in a product that is tailored to the user's needs and operational constraints, rather than forcing them to adapt their tactics, techniques or procedures to suit the product.
