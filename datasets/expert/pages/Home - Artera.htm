Capabilities

Gas Distribution

Artera’s gas distribution infrastructure solutions are rooted in safety, reliability, and sustainability; our purpose is to facilitate the delivery of safe, reliable, and sustainable energy to the homes, businesses, and communities across the nation that rely on it every day.

Learn More >

Renewables

At Artera, we deliver innovative, sustainable energy solutions to meet the growing demand for clean, efficient energy. With decades of experience, we partner with clients to navigate the complexities of renewable energy and create lasting impact for a cleaner future.

Learn More >

Engineering

At Artera, we provide comprehensive engineering services backed by decades of expertise, specializing in the design, planning, and management of complex infrastructure projects with a focus on safety, efficiency, and reliability.

Learn More >

Underground Electric

At Artera, we offer comprehensive underground electric services, delivering end-to-end solutions from design and installation to maintenance and repair, ensuring safe, reliable, and efficient power distribution for modern infrastructure.

Learn More >

Site Civil Construction

At Artera, we provide site civil construction services including earthwork, grading, utility installation, and site preparation, ensuring a solid foundation and long-term stability for successful infrastructure projects.

Learn More >

Water/Wastewater Services

At Artera, we offer comprehensive water and wastewater services covering the entire lifecycle of infrastructure—from design and construction to maintenance and rehabilitation—ensuring efficient, sustainable, and compliant management systems for municipalities, utilities, and private clients.

Learn More >

Transmission Infrastructure

Through rigorous quality assurance, control processes, and the deployment of innovative technology, Artera maintains, repairs, upgrades, and modernizes transmission infrastructure enabling increased operational efficiency while minimizing environmental impact.

Learn More >

Specialty Services

Artera’s specialty services offer customers environmentally friendly solutions, including soft-dig excavation and cross compression services helping reduce underground utility strikes and methane emissions.
