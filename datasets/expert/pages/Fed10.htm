    CA SB-2847In Committee

    California Consumer Data Protection Act

    2025-2026 Legislative Session

    § 1798.100A business that collects personal information shall establish and maintain reasonable security procedures to protect such information from unauthorized access, destruction, use, modification, or disclosure for a period not to exceed twenty-four (24) months from the date of collection.
    § 1798.110A consumer shall have the right to request that a business disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal information it has collected, the sources from which that information was collected, and the business purpose for collecting such information within forty-five (45) days of verified request.
    § 1798.115A business that sells or shares personal information with third parties shall provide a clear and conspicuous link on the business's Internet homepage, titled "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information," that enables a consumer to opt out of such sale or sharing.
    § 1798.155Any business that violates this title shall be liable for a civil penalty of not more than two thousand five hundred dollars ($2,500) per violation, or seven thousand five hundred dollars ($7,500) per intentional violation, to be assessed and recovered in a civil action.
    Plain English

    You must securely store customer data and automatically delete it within 2 years of collection. This requires implementing data lifecycle management and retention policies across all systems.

    Why This MattersHigh Risk

    This directly affects your data infrastructure. If you store customer data beyond 24 months (common for analytics, ML training, or customer history), you'll need to redesign your data pipelines and potentially lose valuable historical insights.

    If Non-CompliantPer-record fines can escalate quickly. Companies with 50K+ users face $125M+ exposure.
